Under the title “Demystifying Quantum Sensing,” we, as the QOOOL Sensing Project, gave a presentation on September 17, 2026, as part of the Business Breakfast at the International Exhibition for Metalworking (AMB) at the Stuttgart Exhibition Center, with the aim of raising awareness of quantum technology within the industry. In our presentation, we showed interested industry representatives how NV-based quantum sensors work and how they are constructed. In particular, we addressed the following questions: “What is the current state of research?”, “What are the potential industrial applications?”, “What advantages do NV-based quantum sensors offer over other magnetic field sensors?”, and “What developments are relevant for companies?”

We were very pleased with the consistently positive feedback we received for looking at quantum sensors from an engineering perspective, and we’ll be back at Messe Stuttgart in a month for the [Quantum Effects 2026](https://www.messe-stuttgart.de/quantum-effects/en/).
