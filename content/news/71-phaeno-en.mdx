On 4 and 5 November 2026, the projects funded under the BMFTR’s ‘Quantum aktiv’ programme came together for a joint closing event at phaeno in Wolfsburg. QOOOL Sensing was also in attendance, as were representatives from Hahn-Schickard, re:edu and the Department of Physics Education at the University of Stuttgart.

![website screenshot](https://cloud.reedu.de/s/8WKNYsWZGTNeLH9/download)

## Three years full of ideas, development, and experimentation

![website screenshot](https://cloud.reedu.de/s/drHnRMfGXf3ybDZ/download)

After three years of project work, the meeting provided an opportunity to present the results of the individual projects, share experiences, and look ahead together. The focus was also on how the ideas, experiments, and educational programs developed can be utilized in the long term and how existing partnerships can be continued. 
For QOOOL Sensing, the main focus was on developing opportunities for the continued use of our experiment kits and learning materials in schools, student labs, and other educational institutions.

## Hands-On Quantum Sensing at phaeno

On November 5, phaeno was transformed into a giant quantum lab for the morning. During the Quantum Action Day, school classes and other interested visitors were able to explore the world of quantum technologies at numerous hands-on stations and in workshops.

![website screenshot](https://cloud.reedu.de/s/QnojGoCxqTZGqS2/download)

![website screenshot](https://cloud.reedu.de/s/eFoqDnStqri2LZt/download)

QOOOL Sensing was also represented with a booth and its own workshops. Together with the visitors, we conducted experiments related to classical and quantum sensor technology. In addition to our QOOOL Fluoro and Magneto kits, there were various demonstrators and experiments on fluorescence to explore. Participants were able to try things out for themselves, observe, and discover what fluorescent diamonds have to do with modern quantum sensors.

![website screenshot](https://cloud.reedu.de/s/KSSMjCd28d5WDny/download)

![website screenshot](https://cloud.reedu.de/s/2JpgGdRxaxmT9W6/download)

It was especially nice to put our projects to use once again directly with the students, answer their questions, and see which experiments sparked their curiosity the most.

## Project completion – and what happens next?

The meeting in Wolfsburg marked the joint conclusion of the “Quantum aktiv” funding programme. However, our work with the ideas and materials developed will continue: we would like to help ensure that the QOOOL experiment kits and learning resources remain accessible to as many educational institutions as possible, even after the project has ended.

We’re taking away lots of fruitful discussions and ideas from Wolfsburg to help us with our next steps. Many thanks to the organising team, the other Quantum aktiv projects and everyone who took part in our experiments and shared their thoughts with us!
