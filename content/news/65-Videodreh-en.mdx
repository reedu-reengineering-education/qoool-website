How do you condense three years of research and development, countless experiments and the world of quantum sensing into a short video? That is exactly what we set out to do for our new QOOOL Sensing project video.

![website screenshot](https://cloud.reedu.de/s/RsQPXEKn9seLirt/download)

Over the past few weeks, we’ve already taken some initial footage of our experiments and demonstrators. On 15 September, we travelled to Hahn-Schickard in Stuttgart for another day of filming. As well as interviews with those involved in the project, the focus there was primarily on our QOOOL kits and the technology behind them.

![website screenshot](https://cloud.reedu.de/s/qfQF5HfnRkCnTfJ/download)

The video traces the journey of QOOOL Sensing: from the idea of bringing quantum sensor technology out of the research laboratory and closer to schools, universities and the general public, right through to the experimental and sensor systems developed as part of the project. Of course, this also covers Fluoro, DIY and Magneto, work with NV centres in diamonds, and the question of how complex quantum sensor technology can be made accessible and experienced in a practical way. Among other things, the exhibition will showcase the QOOOL kits, diamonds, laboratory set-ups and various applications of magnetic field measurement.

![website screenshot](https://cloud.reedu.de/s/D94crQfmm8GoFAg/download)
![website screenshot](https://cloud.reedu.de/s/tW72CPTgDBH5bbn/download)

In the interviews, those involved in the project also discuss how QOOOL Sensing has developed over the course of the project, what each partner has contributed, and what prospects the resulting solutions offer.

![website screenshot](https://cloud.reedu.de/s/Jq9QCqTRWQrByFe/download)
![website screenshot](https://cloud.reedu.de/s/ENQXff95D43Wc6X/download)

This provides a concise overview of several years of QOOOL Sensing, from the initial ideas and prototypes right through to experiments, educational activities and fully functional sensor systems. The video is still being edited. As soon as it’s ready, we’ll of course share it here too.
